# Vapi.ai — Competitor Analysis

## Overview

Vapi is a voice AI platform that provides the infrastructure for developers to build, test, and deploy voice agents. They handle the real-time voice pipeline (speech-to-text, LLM, text-to-speech) and provide APIs for building conversational voice experiences.

## Positioning Relative to Charlotte

**Vapi sells the platform. Charlotte sells the agent.**

Vapi is a lower-level tool: a developer uses Vapi to build a virtual receptionist from scratch. Charlotte IS the virtual receptionist, pre-built, deployed on Arachne, ready to answer phones.

Think of it like: Vapi is Twilio (the building blocks), Charlotte is the answering service you hire.

## Strategic Implications

- Vapi could be a component Charlotte uses (instead of raw Twilio) if their voice pipeline is better
- Vapi customers who want a finished product instead of building one are Charlotte's target market
- Charlotte competes with Vapi's customers' products, not with Vapi itself
- If Vapi launches their own pre-built receptionist agent, that becomes a direct competitor
