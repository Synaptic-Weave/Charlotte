# Playful UI Theme Samples

Here are the visual mockups generated for the three playful UI themes proposed by the UX Architect.

## 1. The "Office Desktop" Theme
A literal skeuomorphic desk metaphor. The left navigation consists of manila folders and a notepad wizard. The right side features a corkboard widget with sticky notes acting as dynamic alerts and missed calls. A coffee cup icon serves as the settings button.

![Office Desktop Theme](/Users/michaelbrown/.gemini/antigravity/brain/aa8af1df-8850-4e75-a469-a13269d36deb/office_desktop_theme_1781003524558.png)

## 2. The Retro Switchboard
A nostalgic 1950s operator switchboard experience. The left side lists active phone lines with LED indicators. The right side is a massive interactive Patch Bay where virtual cables connect from phone lines to AI prompt jacks. It includes brass dials and tactile toggle switches.

![Retro Switchboard Theme](/Users/michaelbrown/.gemini/antigravity/brain/aa8af1df-8850-4e75-a469-a13269d36deb/retro_switchboard_theme_1781003550566.png)

## 3. Holographic Assistant (Dynamic Companion)
This theme brings Charlotte to life as a dynamic, interactive presence. A stylized, glowing orb or friendly abstract shape resides permanently in the UI on the right side, providing conversational tips, displaying real-time audio visualizers, and offering to automate tasks.
